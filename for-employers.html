Questions

Where do candidates apply?

On your website. Each job has an Apply button that opens the link you give us, such as your careers page or application form. We don't collect applications or resumes.

How long does my job stay posted?

Jobs stay live for 30 days. We'll email you before a job expires so you can renew it with one click, or close it early once the role is filled.

Can I post for more than one location?

Yes. Post a separate job for each clinic or office so it shows up in searches near that location. All of your locations appear together on your free employer profile.

Can I edit or close a job after posting?

Yes. From Manage my Jobs you can update the pay, schedule, description or application link at any time, and close a job as soon as you've hired.
